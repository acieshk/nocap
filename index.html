nocap

Content is split into frames that alternate at your display's refresh rate. Each frame is noise. Their mean is the content. Your visual system does the averaging, so you read it and a single screenshot does not. And the text never enters the DOM, so page-reading AI agents get nothing at all.

What this defeats, and what defeats it

ThreatResult
Reflexive Print Screen, Win+Shift+S, Cmd+Shift+4Blocked
DOM-reading AI agents, LLM scrapers, accessibility-tree readersBlocked absolutely
Single-frame OCR and vision-model ingestionBlocked
View Source, Save Page As, Reader mode, Select All and CopyBlocked absolutely
A blur or denoise pass over one captured frameBlocked at block ≥ stroke width
Screen recording, or any burst of framesDefeated
A phone camera pointed at the screenDefeated
DevTools: a breakpoint, a heap snapshot, a canvas dumpDefeated
The line is automated pipeline against targeted attacker. nocap raises the cost of a casual look and removes the value from every surface a machine reads without being told to. It does not defeat someone who has decided to come after the value, because the client belongs to them. The security check runs both defeats for real rather than asking you to take the row above on trust.

Why the page is this grey

#404040 is close to the darkest colour that still has room to carry noise, so the panels blend into the page and the split still works. A near-black page cannot do both: at #0d0d0e the best text colour that still hides the value is #1a1a1a, which nobody can read, and every readable choice leaks 0.53 or worse. If your design is darker than this, put the secret on a panel at this level rather than on the page itself.
