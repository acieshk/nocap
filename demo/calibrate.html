The centre patch flickers; the surround is a solid block of the colour it is supposed to look like. Slide until the centre patch disappears into the surround. You are adjusting the correction itself, so whatever value nulls it is the answer — nothing is fitted afterwards.

← → adjust · Shift for fine steps · Enter to accept · Space to skip · every trial flickers.

The dark trials flicker weakly, and that is physics rather than a fault: in linear light a dark colour has very little headroom, so it can carry very little noise. rgb(64) sits at about 3.5% of full light, which caps the swing there. If the null is hard to find on those, skip them — it also tells you dark backgrounds mask poorly.
