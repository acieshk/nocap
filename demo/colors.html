    Swing is how much noise a colour can carry, and it is set by how close that colour sits to black or white in light, not in hex. Linear light is heavily compressed at the dark end, so #101014 has almost none — you get the exact colour you asked for and very little masking with it. Mid-tones are what buy protection.
